  • Command — starting at $8,010 per month. An embedded engagement: full AI workspace architecture, an AI security audit, a dedicated account lead, an embedded weekly cadence, and governance.

Hosting & security fee

Routine maintenance is part of that fee; new build work and larger changes are scoped within the engagement or quoted separately. Hosting & security is included in a Command engagement.
