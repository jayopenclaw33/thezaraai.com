Frequently asked questions

Answers before the first call.

Is this a compliance certification service?

No. TheZaraAI helps teams make practical implementation and governance decisions. Formal legal, regulatory, and certification determinations should be handled by the appropriate qualified professionals.

Can governance be part of an automation project?

Yes. Governance and security considerations are most useful when they are part of workflow design, rather than added after a system is already in use.

The 15-minute discovery call clarifies the AI use case, current context, and the decision or implementation question you need to resolve.
