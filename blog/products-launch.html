Two New Products Just Dropped — Here's What They Are and Why I Built Them

By Jax ScottPublished March 20, 2026Updated August 10, 20267 min read

Two products just went live — and I want to tell you what they are, who they're actually for, and why I spent months putting them together instead of just posting more content.

Today's a big day. Two products just went live — and I want to tell you what they are, who they're actually for, and why I spent months putting them together instead of just posting more content.

The short version: I got tired of watching smart people undersell themselves with bad AI setups. People with real expertise — years in the field, hard-won knowledge — using AI like it's a fancy Google search. That bothered me. I built these to fix it. It's the same gap our client work closes at the business level; these are the self-serve version.

Here's what dropped today.

Product 1: AI Agent Starter Kit — $27

New release

AI Agent Starter Kit

$27 — one-time, yours forever

Everything you need to turn a generic AI into a personal agent that actually knows who you are — and remembers it next session.

  • SOUL.md — Give your AI a real personality. Not a chatbot. An agent.
  • USER.md — Your background, preferences, goals. It knows you every session.
  • MEMORY.md — It remembers what you built last week. No more starting from zero.
  • 35 curated prompts — Get to useful output fast, without the trial and error.
  • Troubleshooting guide — Fix the most common issues before they slow you down.

Get the Starter Kit — $27 →

This kit is for anyone who's been using ChatGPT, Claude, or OpenClaw and feeling like they're not getting everything out of it. The dirty secret? The tool is usually fine. The setup isn't. Most people never configure their AI with any context about who they are, what they do, or what they're working on. Every session starts completely cold.

SOUL.md, USER.md, and MEMORY.md are the three files that change that. They're not complicated — they're structured text files that live in your workspace and load automatically every time your agent wakes up. (I walk through the free version of this setup in how to give your AI agent a real memory.) The result is an AI that feels less like a tool and more like a collaborator. One that knows your voice, your work, your preferences. One that compounds instead of resetting.

I use this exact system myself — with Zara — and the difference between a Zara session and a cold ChatGPT session is not subtle. She knows my background. She knows my projects. She knows how I write. That's what the Starter Kit builds for you.

  • Zero Trust & CMMC — Policy docs, gap analysis, compliance frameworks

Get the Prompt Pack — $47 →

This one's for the cybersecurity professionals. And I need to be direct with you about something: I spent a long time wrestling with whether to release this, because I've seen too many "prompt packs" that are just generic filler dressed up in a domain costume.

This is not that.

These 100 prompts are the ones I actually use. Built and tested over 19 years in the field — U.S. Army Special Forces cyber, red team work, compliance, incident response, executive advisory. The kind of work where vague, generic prompts don't just waste time — they produce outputs you can't trust. These are engineered for specificity. They give AI the context it needs to produce work that's actually useful in a real security environment. (For the thinking behind them, read how to use AI for cybersecurity without getting burned.)

The most underrated thing in this pack is the executive reporting section. If you've ever spent four hours writing a findings brief that a VP glanced at for 30 seconds, you know exactly why I built that category. The right prompts cut that time dramatically — and produce outputs that are actually formatted for the humans who need to read them.

Why I Built These (The Real Version)

I've been in cybersecurity for almost two decades. I've watched the field change through every major technology shift. Cloud, mobile, IoT, and now AI. Every time, there's a window where early adopters get a serious edge — and then it closes, and everyone catches up, and the edge is gone.

We're in that window right now with AI.

Most security professionals are still treating AI like a novelty. They'll paste in a paragraph and ask it to "make this better." Maybe they'll ask it to summarize a CVE. That's fine — but it leaves most of the capability on the table. The people who figure out how to actually build AI into their workflow — who set up a real agent and give it the right context — are doing significantly better work in significantly less time.

I built these products to help close that gap. Not just for security people — the Starter Kit is for anyone — but especially for the people I know best: the ones who've put years into their expertise and deserve tools that honor that expertise instead of dumbing it down.

No subscriptions. No upsells. Pay once, own it forever. That was a deliberate choice. I'm building products I'd actually want to buy.
